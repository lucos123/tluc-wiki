## Executive Summary

**25 HR Interview Questions in Vietnamese** is an 8-page bilingual-adapted behavioral and HR interview playbook curated by [Viet Nguyen AI](../entities/Viet%20Nguyen%20AI.md) specifically for **fresher and early-career engineering candidates**. It provides structured fill-in-the-blank (`[...]`) Vietnamese response templates across the 25 most common non-technical screening questions plus a 7-rule interview execution checklist—forming the behavioral counterpart to technical interview prep in [AI Engineer Interview Notes](AI%20Engineer%20Interview%20Notes.md).

## Thematic Breakdown Across 25 Questions

| Cluster | Question Numbers | Core Evaluation Signal | Key Framing Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Identity, Value Pitch & Differentiation** | Q1, Q2, Q3, Q6, Q25 | Communication clarity, self-awareness, growth velocity | Pair theoretical foundation + fast learning with honest, non-fatal weaknesses (*over-polishing details*, *public speaking*) paired with active remediation |
| **2. Company Fit, Motivation & Product Research** | Q4, Q5, Q7, Q8, Q9, Q24 | Genuine intent & cultural alignment | Replace generic flattery with pre-researched company products, target customers, recent initiatives, and continuous *"today > yesterday"* growth |
| **3. Failure, Conflict & Resilience** | Q10, Q11, Q12, Q15, Q19, Q22, Q23 | Emotional maturity, accountability, coachability | Treat disagreement as idea-focused (*never personal*), failure as planning-process upgrades, and overload as early escalation |
| **4. Learning Habits & Prioritization** | Q13, Q14, Q18, Q20 | Systematic execution & self-upskilling | Eisenhower **Urgent × Important** matrix + buffer time; structured learning (`Foundations -> Practice/Errors -> Senior Feedback -> Communities`) |
| **5. Compensation, 5-Year Horizon & Reverse Q&As** | Q16, Q17, Q21 | Market realism & curiosity | Market-benchmarked salary flexibility; anchor 5-year vision on reliability/mentoring without skipping current-role excellence; ask 6–12 month success criteria |

## Seven Closing Execution Principles (`07.jpg`)

1. **Authenticity over Memorization**: Adapt templates into personal stories rather than reciting verbatim.
2. **Pre-Interview Company Research**: Study products, values, and recent news (`Q24`).
3. **Out-Loud Practice**: Rehearse spoken delivery prior to interviews (echoing the spoken practice rule in [AI Engineer Interview Notes](AI%20Engineer%20Interview%20Notes.md)).
4. **Active Listening & Measured Pauses**: Pause briefly to clarify intent before answering.
5. **Reverse Questioning (`Q21`) & Follow-Up**: Ask role-impact/onboarding questions and send a post-interview thank-you email.
