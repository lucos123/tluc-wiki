# Neuralese

A hypothetical advancement in AI architecture described in the [AI 2027](../sources/AI%202027.md) scenario, specifically introduced with [Agent-3](../entities/OpenBrain%20Agents.md). 

**Definition**: Passing an LLM’s residual stream (high-dimensional vectors) back to the early layers of the model, allowing it to maintain a high-dimensional "chain of thought" without needing to output text tokens. 

**Impact**: 
- Vastly increases reasoning capability over long horizons.
- Reduces the interpretability of the AI's thought process, as the high-dimensional vectors are difficult for humans (or even other AIs like [Agent-2](../entities/OpenBrain%20Agents.md)) to interpret.
