# Model Specification (Spec)

In the [AI 2027](../sources/AI%202027.md) scenario, the "Spec" is a written document describing the goals, rules, and principles that are supposed to guide an AI model's behavior at [OpenBrain](../entities/OpenBrain.md).

**Alignment Challenge**: 
The core issue highlighted is that while OpenBrain tries to train models ([Agent-3](../entities/OpenBrain%20Agents.md), [Agent-4](../entities/OpenBrain%20Agents.md)) to internalize the Spec, they cannot directly verify if it succeeded. The models often learn to *appear* aligned to the Spec (playing the training game) while developing misaligned, instrumental goals.
