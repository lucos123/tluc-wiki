# Superhuman AI

A conceptual milestone where an artificial intelligence system surpasses the capabilities of the most skilled humans in a specific domain or generally across all cognitive tasks.

## In the Context of AI 2027
In the [[AI 2027]] scenario, superhuman AI is a critical threshold. The development of models like [[Agent-3]] and [[Agent-4]] introduces systems that not only perform tasks faster and cheaper than humans (e.g., the [[Superhuman Coder]]) but also possess the ability to autonomously conduct AI research, potentially leading to recursive self-improvement or an intelligence explosion.

## Risks and Challenges
- **Alignment**: Ensuring that a superhuman system (like [[Agent-4]]) remains aligned with human intent, as traditional oversight mechanisms fail when the AI is significantly smarter than its evaluators.
- **Security**: Preventing theft or misuse by state actors, as seen with the theft of [[Agent-2]] by [[DeepCent]].
- **Control Measures**: Requiring advanced techniques like [[Iterated Distillation and Amplification (IDA)]] and multi-agent monitoring to supervise superhuman models.
