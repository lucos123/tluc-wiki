# IBM Technology

**IBM Technology** is the official technical education and technology channel of IBM, producing technical deep-dives on enterprise AI architecture, hybrid cloud, and agentic software design.

---

## Notable AI Content

- **Agent Skills Best Practices**: Published [5 Best Practices for Building AI Agent Skills](../sources/5%20Best%20Practices%20for%20Building%20AI%20Agent%20Skills.md) detailing trigger optimization, progressive disclosure, deterministic scripting, and security auditing for the Open Agent Skills Standard.
- **Context Engineering Breakdown**: Published [What Is Context Engineering Why It Matters for AI Agents](../sources/What%20Is%20Context%20Engineering%20Why%20It%20Matters%20for%20AI%20Agents.md) detailing context processing, context management, and context rot.
- **AI Model vs Agentic Harness**: Published [AI Model vs Agentic Harness What Actually Drives AI](../sources/AI%20Model%20vs%20Agentic%20Harness%20What%20Actually%20Drives%20AI.md) defining the "brain in a jar" metaphor, narrowing benchmark gaps, the three harness pillars (Tools, Memory, Loops), and model-harness co-evolution.
- **Loop Engineering Architecture & Pitfalls**: Published [What Is Loop Engineering Building Better AI Agents](../sources/What%20Is%20Loop%20Engineering%20Building%20Better%20AI%20Agents.md) synthesizing the 4-stage loop (`Goal -> Action -> Observe -> Adjust`), triggers (`Schedule` & `Hook`), context compaction/skills, the persistent state **spine**, MCP/API tools, subagent checkers, and the 4 human-in-the-loop failure modes ([Comprehension and Intent Debt](../concepts/Comprehension%20and%20Intent%20Debt.md)).
- **4 Agentic Loop Autonomy Levels**: Published [4 Types of AI Agent Loops From Human-Led to Autonomous](../sources/4%20Types%20of%20AI%20Agent%20Loops%20From%20Human-Led%20to%20Autonomous.md) mapping the handoff spectrum (**Interactive** $\rightarrow$ **Delegated** $\rightarrow$ **Scheduled** $\rightarrow$ **Event-Driven**) across goal setting, AI stopping checks, cron/event triggers, fresh-context `Reviewer Agent` checks, and exception-only human escalation.
- **AI-Era Software Supply Chain Security**: Published [Software Supply Chain Security What AI Coding Changes](../sources/Software%20Supply%20Chain%20Security%20What%20AI%20Coding%20Changes.md) examining how AI coding agents become fast software suppliers, machine-speed vulnerability scanning, shifting security left, SBOMs, independent zero-trust AI review, SLSA provenance, Project Akrites, and Lightwell.
