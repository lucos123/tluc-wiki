# Uber

Uber is a global mobility and food delivery ($90B run rate) technology enterprise.

## AI Engineering Practice

- **Multimodal Agent Orchestration**: Deploys vision-language agents (VLMs) across 10,000 cities to selectively route, edit, and verify merchant food photography on Uber Eats.
- **Closed-Loop Evals**: Operates production auto-tuning pipelines where a Diagnosis Agent and Reflect/Synthesize sub-agents update model prompts and configs automatically based on human golden datasets and production feedback.

See [Building Closed-Loop Evals for a Multimodal Agent at Scale](../sources/Building%20Closed-Loop%20Evals%20for%20a%20Multimodal%20Agent%20at%20Scale.md) and [Closed-Loop Evals](../concepts/Closed-Loop%20Evals.md).
