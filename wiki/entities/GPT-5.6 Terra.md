# GPT-5.6 Terra

`gpt-5.6-terra` is the balanced tier model in [OpenAI](OpenAI.md)'s [GPT-5.6](GPT-5.6.md) family, designed to balance high intelligence and cost.

## Specifications

- **Context & Output**: 1.05M context window, 128K max output tokens. Knowledge cutoff Feb 16, 2026.
- **Pricing**: $2.00 / 1M input, $0.20 / 1M cached input, $12.00 / 1M output tokens.
- **Role**: Everyday workhorse model replacing legacy `gpt-5.4` and `gpt-5.5` models at a fraction of the cost.

See [GPT-5.6 Terra Model API Reference](../sources/GPT-5.6%20Terra%20Model%20API%20Reference.md) and [GPT-5.6](GPT-5.6.md).
