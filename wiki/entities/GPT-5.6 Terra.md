# GPT-5.6 Terra

`gpt-5.6-terra` is the balanced tier model in [[OpenAI]]'s [[GPT-5.6]] family, designed to balance high intelligence and cost.

## Specifications

- **Context & Output**: 1.05M context window, 128K max output tokens. Knowledge cutoff Feb 16, 2026.
- **Pricing**: $2.00 / 1M input, $0.20 / 1M cached input, $12.00 / 1M output tokens.
- **Role**: Everyday workhorse model replacing legacy `gpt-5.4` and `gpt-5.5` models at a fraction of the cost.

See [[GPT-5.6 Terra Model API Reference]] and [[GPT-5.6]].
