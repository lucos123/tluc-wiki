# AI Engineer

**AI Engineer** is a global community, technical conference series (AI Engineer World's Fair, AI Engineer Summit), and knowledge-sharing organization focused on the emerging discipline of AI engineering, agentic systems, LLMOps, and production AI architectures.

## Key Sources & Ingested Works

- [[Why Your Enterprise Tech Stack Isn't Ready for AI Agents]]: Technical presentation by Christopher Lovejoy and Saul Howard ([[Anterior]]) on the 4 architectural primitives for enterprise agent stacks, the immutable action ledger, and Human-Agent Equivalency.
- [[All Code Is Slop (Except Mine) — Lauren Tan, SpaceXSI]]: AI Engineer Code Summit presentation by [[Lauren Tan]] (`@poteto`, Principal Engineer at SpaceX / Grok Bot) introducing the [[Poteto Trust Pyramid]], deterministic lint-over-prose refactoring (`167` custom rules), runtime feature maps/CLIs, formal/UI verification (`TLA+`, `Lean`, `Bombadil`), and scaling to `11,000 PRs/month` at a `~0.5%` revert rate.
