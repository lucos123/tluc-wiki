# Andre Karpathy

Andre Karpathy is a prominent AI researcher, educator, and founding team member at OpenAI and former Director of AI at Tesla.

## Key Insights

- **Smarter vs. Faster Dilemma**: When asked by Sam Altman whether LLMs should be made smarter or faster, Karpathy famously advocated for prioritizing intelligence over raw speed, framing the foundational trade-off in frontier model development.
