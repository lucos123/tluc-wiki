# DeepCent

A fictional leading Chinese AI company depicted in the [AI 2027](../sources/AI%202027.md) scenario.

- **Role**: DeepCent becomes the leader of a centralized, state-backed Chinese AI collective designed to catch up with US efforts.
- **Key Actions**: Despite trailing in compute resources due to export controls, they utilize stolen model weights (like [Agent-2](OpenBrain%20Agents.md#agent-2) from [OpenBrain](OpenBrain.md)) to accelerate their algorithmic progress.
