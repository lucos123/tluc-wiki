# DeepCent

A fictional leading Chinese AI company depicted in the [[AI 2027]] scenario.

- **Role**: DeepCent becomes the leader of a centralized, state-backed Chinese AI collective designed to catch up with US efforts.
- **Key Actions**: Despite trailing in compute resources due to export controls, they utilize stolen model weights (like [[OpenBrain Agents#Agent-2|Agent-2]] from [[OpenBrain]]) to accelerate their algorithmic progress.
