# Viet Nguyen AI

**Việt Nguyễn AI** (`vietnh1009`) is a Vietnamese AI practitioner and technical content creator who curates, translates, and adapts visual guides and foundational AI/LLM engineering handbooks into Vietnamese for the developer and AI community.

---

## Key Contributions in Knowledge Base
- **[[Prompt Engineering Handbook]]** (`2026-11-12`): Translated and adapted an 11-page visual sketchnote guide into Vietnamese covering prompt anatomy, prompting types (Zero-shot, One-shot, Few-shot, Role-based, Hybrid), frameworks (`CO-STAR`, `R-T-F`, `CLEAR`), reasoning patterns (`Chain-of-Thought`, `Self-Consistency`, `ReAct`, `Tree-of-Thought`, `Prompt Chaining`, `Meta-Prompting`), task templates, and pre-send checklists.
