# Cerebras Systems

Cerebras Systems is a semiconductor hardware company specializing in wafer-scale AI processors (such as the WSE-3 chip) designed for ultra-low-latency model inference and training.

## Partnership with OpenAI

- **$10B 750 MW Deal**: In 2026, OpenAI entered into a $10 Billion, 30-month infrastructure agreement with Cerebras to access 750 MW of data center compute through 2028.
- **750 Tokens/Second Inference**: Powers the ultra-fast serving tier of OpenAI's flagship model `[[GPT-5.6]]` Sol, delivering an 18.5x speedup over standard GPU clusters.
