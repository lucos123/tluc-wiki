# Leonhard Euler

Leonhard Euler (1707–1783) was a Swiss polymath and mathematician who founded **Graph Theory** in 1736 by solving the Seven Bridges of Königsberg problem.

## Relevance to Agentic Engineering

Euler's mathematical abstraction of landmasses as *nodes* and bridges as *edges* forms the theoretical foundation for **[[Graph Engineering]]**, where agent execution environments (nodes) and control/data flows (edges) are structured into DAGs or cyclic graphs.
